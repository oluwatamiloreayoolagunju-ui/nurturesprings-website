GET TO KNOW US

Growing Greatness, One Child at a Time

At NurtureSprings, we believe every child is special and has the potential to be great, given the right care, enablement and support.

Founded in Lagos, Nigeria, NurtureSprings is a child development and education brand built on one promise: nurturing children to become confident, kind, literate and digitally safe individuals.

We do not just teach children to pass examinations. We nurture them to become confident, compassionate and responsible individuals who are ready for school and ready for life.
