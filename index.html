GET TO KNOW US

Growing Greatness, One Child at a Time

At NurtureSprings, we believe every child is special and has the potential to be great, given the right care, enablement and support.

Founded in Lagos, Nigeria, NurtureSprings is a child development and education brand built on one promise: nurturing children to become confident, kind, literate and digitally safe individuals.

We do not just teach children to pass examinations. We nurture them to become confident, compassionate and responsible individuals who are ready for school and ready for life.

Meet Our Founder

Dr. Isioma Stella Olagunju. PhD (Arts), PhD (Early Childhood Education)University Lecturer, Lagos State University | Founder, NurtureSprings | Convener, Bring Back Handwriting Movement

Founder, NurtureSprings

Dr. Isioma Stella Olagunju is a University Lecturer at Lagos State University (Early Childhood Education Unit), Global Education Consultant, Handwriting Expert, Early Years Literacy Specialist, and Indigenous Literacy and Child's Right Advocate. An alumna of the University of Ilorin and Lagos State University, she holds a rare dual doctorate — a PhD in Arts and a PhD in Early Childhood Education. She brings together the artistry of the written word and the science of how children learn. She is the Founder of NurtureSprings Book Club and NurtureSprings Handwriting Academy and the Convener of 'Bring Back Handwriting'— a pioneering movement committed to restoring handwriting as a core skill for learning, excellence, and intellectual discipline. An author of children's literature, she has served as Lecturer at Ajayi Crowther University, Chair of the Ajayi Crowther University School Board, and Principal of Grace High School, Gbagada, Lagos. With over three decades of experience, she has shaped generations of learners, teachers, and parents. Under her leadership, NurtureSprings delivers premium assessment and intervention programmes for children aged 6-18 and interested adults, including the Pre-Writing Readiness Excellence Assessment for children below age 6. Her approach is precise, personalised, and results-driven — helping children write with clarity, confidence, and character. Her philosophy is simple and powerful: Build, do not break. Prepare, do not pressure. Get them rooted, not rushed. Handwriting is a lifelong skill.Our Motto: Nurture • Grow • Thrive Our Anchor:In Thy Mercy Shall We Thrive.

Meet Our Chairman

Prof. Gbadebo Anthony Olagunju LL.B, LL.M, B.L, PhD. Chairman, NurtureSprings

Chairman, NurtureSprings

Prof. Gbadebo Anthony Olagunju is a Professor of Law at Lagos State University, in the Department of International and Islamic Law, Faculty of Law. He is a Solicitor and Advocate of the Supreme Court of Nigeria. He holds the LL.B and LL.M degrees of the University of Ife, now Obafemi Awolowo University, a PhD from St. Augustine University of Tanzania, and the Barrister at Law (B.L) professional certificate of the Nigerian Law School. A distinguished scholar and academic leader, he has served as Sub-Dean, Faculty of Law, Head of the Department of Jurisprudence and International Law, and Head of the Department of International and Islamic Law at Lagos State University. He is a former Visiting Research Fellow at the Max Planck Institute for Comparative Public Law and International Law, Heidelberg, Germany, and a Fellow of the Salzburg Global Seminar, Austria. His scholarship spans International Law, Maritime and Admiralty Law, International Humanitarian Law, and the Nigerian Legal System, with widely cited works on maritime liens, ship arrest, safety of navigation, and the protection of non-military objects in armed conflict. As Chairman of NurtureSprings, Prof. Olagunju provides vision, governance, and integrity to the mission — ensuring that every child is nurtured with excellence, discipline, and character. He stands for what NurtureSprings stands for: Nurture • Grow • Thrive In Thy Mercy Shall We Thrive.
